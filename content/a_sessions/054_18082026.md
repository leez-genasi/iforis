The party stumbled on to the inauguration of a new church to Tymora. Arkansas led the party over with joy and excitement, even partaking in the blessings provided by the fellow priests and priestesses of the Church.  
The head priest pulls Arkansas aside, introduces himself as Luckbringer (Term for priests and priestess of Tymora) Yuldrin. He is in charge of the church. He ushers Arkansas into his office and engages in some small talk (Life, Travel, Troubles, Etc)  
Eventually Luckbringer Yuldrin opens the topic about Arkansas’ past. He knows about Arkansas, a child of the church, orphaned and separated by his parents at a young and tender age. A child who sought to give his all to the one whom had realised his potential and inspired him throughout his youth. Goddess Tymora. But the strings of the multiverse had better plans.  
Luckbringer Yuldrin explains Arkansas' predicament, though some would insult him and call it a gullible trap, Yuldrin sees it as a gift, and a silver lining.  
“Those who follow the light alone, will only see what they want to see. But those who sought the light, but found the darkness from the light will be the ones who guide the path ahead - and that means you”  
“You have discovered your new potential through these many years, your patron has laid dormant, watching, biding their time and now they have made themselves known to you. Likely you may have felt deceived, lost, betrayed, confused. But fret not, you are already halfway through your journey, the road ahead is paved and bright.”  
Yuldrin explains the history of Moander and Tymora. A tale that speaks about the two beings of different eras, but in a way, Tymora would only exist because of Moander. Yuldrin refamiliarises Arkansas to Beshaba, the goddess of misfortune and Sister to Tymora.  
“Their origin runs deep from Moander’s curse eons ago, the pieces of this puzzle have been lost in our records, but you may rediscover them again. Head to the high temple of Beshaba, speak with the head Doommasters (Term for priests and priestess of Beshaba) of the temple. They may be able to share the history of our two goddesses.”  
“I implore you to proceed on this quest, not because we wish to fill our archives with knowledge and history but because I received a premonition a year ago from today. Our goddess sent word that an individual dazed and dancing on the blessings of two omnipotent entities would appear and visit this church on the day it was completed. I held on to that message, and I believe she refers to you.”  
“She left one final message - The balance between fortune and misfortune need not be in the hands of two…”  
Luckbringer Yuldrin answers as many questions as he can from Arkansas. He believes that the ones chosen by Moander are not just based on a game of chance.
