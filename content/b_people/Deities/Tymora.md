### Tymora v. Beshaba
Beshaba is the opposite of Tymora. Followers of Tymora is thankful to the Goddess that the luck lands in their favor. Followers of Beshaba are thankful when luck doesn't land in their favor and how do they naviagte. Tymora teaches people to embrace opportunities and good fortune. Beshaba teaches followers to endure adversity

Neither side will agree with one another. One would rather improve themself through adversity, the other would rather fortune to create opportunities for themselves. (Two contrasting philosophical ideals)

#### Overlaps in the Cultures
- Both look toward chance + how should they proceed
	- Some are multireligious and it's good to curry favor both sides (neither lose or win) > more common, realistic ideals
	- Most people would choose either one, won't switch over, because this particular goddess fits their ideals better

### Tyche (Goddess of Destinty, Prosperity)
There used to be followers of Tyche. After the battle where Moander poisoned Tyche, Tyche had to be split into two (by Selune) to save her. 
Moander and Tyche used to have beef (they lived on the same plane, and used to be mortals)
- Correlation btw Moander and Tyche:
	- Moander used to be misfortune, Tyche was fortune
	- Then Moander changed to rot, strayed away from misfortune, removed herself from the chance domain, Tyche became the one that embodies both.
	- Why was there beef?
		- Tyche was supposed to defeat Moander, but because Tyche failed, so Tymora felt that it fell on her to defeat Moander
		- Beshaba wants nothing with the beef
		- So they fought for a while, and there's a bit of the truce > Moander takes the follower every few years or so BUT there is something else. 

How was Tyche and Moander became deities:
- Tyche was chosen by a goddess from another plane/planet after Tyche was trying to escape from some allies that betrayed him
- Moander: someone who was interested in unnatural things (rot, disease, decomposition, destruction, afterlife). Through her prayers, a particular deity came to her and groomed her into a deity. Started out as misfortune (because Death was related to misfortune)
