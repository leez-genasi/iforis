### Citadel

- Dedicated to Auril, Goddess of Winter
- Lightly guarded
- Cushion floor with symbol of Auril
- They sell Auril amulets, each as unique as a snowflake
- Architecture-wise, outside looks like it's 3 floors, but inside only has headspace for 2 floors
  - There was a magically locked door which led to a third floor
  - A ring of cold resistance was hidden in the roof of the third floor
